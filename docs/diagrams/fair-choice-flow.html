Why this is fair
Every round rotates who's first (P1 goes to the back). Losing a contest raises your contestedLossCounter, which is the top sort key next time you're in a contest — so unlucky heirs get first crack at what they want most, and no heir stays unlucky. High-value items never enter this loop; the trustee handles them separately on the Record of Decisions.
