# Registry — Sign screen v3 & memorandum language sweep (2026-08-09)

**Status:** Shipped. Tests green. Reshot.
**Related:** `docs/handoffs/2026-08-09-commit-4b.md`, `docs/specs/2026-08-09-registry-couple-mode.md`

## What changed and why

Owner feedback: the previous sign screen implied Legacy Registry was
issuing a legal document. It is not, and it must not present itself that
way. This slice removes that framing throughout the owner-facing UI and
collapses the sign screen from a two-input form to a single typed-phrase
confirmation.

### Sign screen (`giftsign`)

- **Screen title:** `Confirm your choices` (was `Sign the memorandum`).
- **Body copy** rewritten to say plainly:
  - "This is not a legal document, and Legacy Registry is not a legal or
    fiduciary service. What matters is that you print your list, sign the
    paper with a pen, and get it to whoever holds your will."
  - "On the next screen, Legacy Registry can email an unsigned preview…
    That email is a courtesy, not a filing."
  - "Life changes. You can come back any time and confirm a new version —
    the newest one is the one that counts. You may never need to, and
    that is fine."
- **Inputs:** the "your full legal name" input was **removed**. There is
  now one input, labelled `Type These are my wishes today to confirm`.
  The typed phrase is the confirmation evidence; the owner's identity is
  already established by the account and the printed paper carries the
  handwritten signature.
- **Button:** `Confirm my choices` (was `Sign the memorandum`).
- **Validator:** the acknowledgement string is now
  `These are my wishes today` (case-insensitive), replacing
  `I sign this memorandum today`.

### Signed versions screen (`giftversions`)

- Lede updated: "Every time you confirm your list, a new version is
  filed. The newest one is the one that counts. Life changes — come back
  any time and confirm a new version."
- Version card meta: `Confirmed <date>` (was `Signed <date>`).
- Removed the `Signed by <name>` line — there is no longer a client-side
  name input to display.
- Email-preview offer: "This is a **courtesy, not a filing** — the paper
  you sign by hand and get to them is what carries the weight" (was
  "not legally binding — the signed paper is what carries the weight").
- Empty state: `You have not confirmed your list yet. Come back after
  your first confirmation.`

## Next slice — Couple Mode

The user's follow-up ("each user of the couple needs their own page to
validate and send") means Couple Mode is the next slice. Direction locked
in this conversation:

- **Not shared account with fast-switch.** The user wants each spouse on
  their **own device**. Each builds their own list of special gifting on
  their own device, signs their own addendum, delivers to their own
  wills-storage caretaker.
- Household inventory (jointly-decided items that aren't in either
  addendum) remains shared and delivers at second death — this matches
  what `docs/specs/2026-08-09-registry-couple-mode.md` already describes.
- **The spec's fast-switch model in §"Two-participant sign-in" needs
  updating** to reflect the two-device direction. Do that at the start
  of the next slice before touching code.
- Zero Couple Mode code exists today: `REGISTRY_OWNER_PARTICIPANT_ID` is
  a single constant `'owner'`, the schema has no `household` table, and
  the item repo does not filter by which participant added an item.
  Everything is single-owner from the routing layer down.
